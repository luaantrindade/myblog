Getting Started with AI‑Assisted Support

Sep 28, 2026

Artificial Intelligence is transforming technical support. By integrating LLMs into your ticketing system, you can:

  • Automatically categorize incoming tickets
  • Suggest knowledge‑base articles
  • Generate draft responses for agents
  • Reduce average resolution time by up to 30%

To get started:

  1. Choose an LLM API (OpenAI, Anthropic, or an open‑source model).
  2. Create a simple webhook that receives new ticket data.
  3. Prompt the model with the ticket description and ask for classification and suggested reply.
  4. Post the suggestions back to your ticketing system as internal notes.
  5. Monitor performance and fine‑tune prompts.

Remember to keep human oversight—AI assists, not replaces, the support engineer.
